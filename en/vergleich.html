Where SpeechPilot doesn't win

Dragon, once trained, is the most accurate on difficult vocabulary; Yaptxt processes everything on the device and works in any program, not just the browser; Voice In has 600,000 installs and years of head start. SpeechPilot is built for one case: chat shifts in the browser, with profile fields and standard replies next to the box. If you need something else, you're better served elsewhere.

Check it yourself instead of taking my word: Basic is free, Complete is 14 days without a card.
