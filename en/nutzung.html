Speech recognition

Recognition is performed by the browser: Chrome processes the audio on Google servers. The provider has no influence over that.

Recognition runs in the extension's own area, not in the page you are visiting: that page sees neither the audio nor the recognised text. Microphone permission is therefore granted once to SpeechPilot itself rather than to every page anew — Chrome asks for it in the address bar the first time you switch the microphone on.
